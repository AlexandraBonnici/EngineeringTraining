11:05–12:05 · WORKSHOP 1 · ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

Engineering a robot’s journey

From the physics of wheel motion to the mathematics of turning and programming a VEX robot to reach its target.

01 · UNDERSTAND

How does a robot move?

Relate wheel circumference and rotation to travel distance; explore friction and the forces driving the robot forwards.

02 · MODEL

How does it turn?

Use opposing wheel motions, torque, track width and geometry to predict robot turns, including a 90° rotation.

03 · PROGRAMME

Can it reach the target?

Configure a VEX drivetrain, measure the robot, programme straight movement and turns, then test a route from A to B.

01 · WHEEL MOTION

Predict forward travel

Measure wheel circumference and connect one wheel rotation to distance. Discuss traction and friction.

02 · TURNING

Reason about rotation

Explore forces, torque, track width and wheel rotations needed for a right-angle turn.

03 · VEX PYTHON

Configure and programme

Set motor directions, describe wheel circumference, track width and wheelbase, and command a forward motion and 90° turn.

04 · CHALLENGE

Reach the target

Plan, programme and test a route from A to B; compare commanded motion with actual movement.

12:15–13:15 · WORKSHOP 2 · MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

Lightweight engineering: from design to microscopy

A hands-on introduction to designing lightweight components for additive manufacturing, preparing lattice samples for characterisation and examining them using electron microscopy.

01 · DESIGN

Design a lightweight component

Explore how lattice structures can reduce weight, and how component geometry and manufacturing constraints influence design for additive manufacturing.

02 · PREPARE

Prepare samples for characterisation

Work through the preparation of additively manufactured lattice samples, learning why careful preparation is essential for reliable microscopic examination.

03 · EXAMINE

Explore through electron microscopy

Examine the prepared samples using electron microscopy to reveal fine details of the material and its manufactured features.
